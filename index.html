研究方向Research Focus

"我试图理解，当环境发生变化时，生命如何在生理、行为与演化的多重尺度上做出回应。" "I seek to understand how life responds — physiologically, behaviourally, and evolutionarily — when environments change."

我的研究专注于演化生态学与保护生理学，横跨爬行类、两栖类与鱼类等多个类群。我关心的问题既有基础理论价值，也具备保护应用意义：物种如何感知环境变化？表型可塑性能否帮助种群渡过快速变化的气候？这些问题指导着我的野外与实验室工作。 My research focuses on evolutionary ecology and conservation physiology, spanning reptiles, amphibians, and fish. The questions I care about carry both fundamental theoretical value and applied conservation relevance: how do species perceive environmental change? Can phenotypic plasticity help populations weather rapid climatic shifts? These questions guide both my fieldwork and lab work.

01 · TOPIC

热耐受与气候变化Thermal Tolerance & Climate Change

研究生物如何调节体温、代谢与行为以应对升温环境，预测种群在气候变化下的脆弱性。 Studying how organisms regulate body temperature, metabolism, and behaviour under warming conditions, to predict population vulnerability to climate change.

02 · TOPIC

发育可塑性Developmental Plasticity

探究早期发育环境如何塑造成年个体的表型、生理机能与长期适应能力。 Investigating how early developmental environments shape adult phenotype, physiology, and long-term adaptive capacity.

03 · TOPIC

行为与性选择演化Behaviour & Sexual Selection

结合野外行为观测与分子数据，理解性选择如何驱动表型多样性的产生与维持。 Combining field behavioural observation with molecular data to understand how sexual selection drives and maintains phenotypic diversity.

04 · TOPIC

保护生理学Conservation Physiology

将生理学工具应用于濒危物种保护实践，为管理决策提供科学依据。 Applying physiological tools to endangered species conservation, providing scientific evidence for management decisions.
